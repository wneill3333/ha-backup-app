Home Assistant Backups

This is a private, personal app. It lets one household's Home Assistant server save its own encrypted backup files to the owner's own Google Drive. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy policy

The app only creates, reads and deletes the backup files it uploads itself (Google Drive "drive.file" access). It does not read any other files, and no data is shared with, sold to, or sent to anyone else. Backups stay in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's own equipment. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
